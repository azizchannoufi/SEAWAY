Our story

Born from the moment you step out of the water.

Cold skin, salty hair, a smile you can't hide. Seaway began with that feeling and a simple wish: a warm, free and authentic wardrobe for what comes after the session, inspired by the Mediterranean and the craft of the traditional fouta.
